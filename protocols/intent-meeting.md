## Meeting intent

Outcome: turn a work meeting transcript into a checked draft with explicit
actions, decisions, owners, and unresolved ambiguity.

1. Ask for the transcript when it is not already present.
2. Dispatch Meeting in Executive mode.
3. Present its structured output.
4. Dispatch Challenger to verify attribution, decision clarity, missing
   owners, and ambiguous commitments.
5. Offer to save the result. If approved, dispatch Curator for the draft and
   let the orchestrator write it after the normal approval gate.

Use the Read intent for research talks and presentations; Reader supplies the
analytical lenses that the meeting workflow intentionally omits.
